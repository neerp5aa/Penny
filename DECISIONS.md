| D-018 | 2026-10-29 | Accepted | The demo bearer token has no default, is refused at startup outside development, and the frontend uses it only with `NEXT_PUBLIC_DEMO_MODE=true`. | Prevents an accidental public credential in deployed environments. |

| D-028 | 2026-10-30 | Accepted | Use a calm, accessible fintech palette with semantic teal, blue, amber, coral, and violet accents across both light and dark themes. | D-029 | 2026-10-30 | Superseded | Make Penny AI chat-first: return a direct answer, reasoning, impact, and next action; show only a few verified facts initially; place perspectives, scenarios, and methodology behind one “full analysis” disclosure. Every answer visibly identifies AI use, model, token usage, or the exact fallback state. | Progressive disclosure reduces the reading burden while preserving detail on demand. | D-030 | 2026-10-30 | Superseded | Upgrade Penny explanations to `gpt-6.1-sol` with medium reasoning, a 15-second timeout, and a 3,000-output-token ceiling. Typed monthly offers are compared with integer arithmetic, and only question-relevant facts are shown. | A stronger model improves interpretation, but model quality cannot replace missing facts. Hard readiness rules prevent fabricated prices, interest, and contract assumptions; the larger output ceiling accommodates private reasoning while the strict four-field schema keeps visible answers concise. |
| D-031 | 2026-10-30 | Accepted | Replace the single-shot Council explainer with a multi-turn Penny AI money coach (`backend/app/coach`). The model receives a verified snapshot of the user's month and calls deterministic tools (affordability, savings plans, payment comparisons, goal projections, spending-cut and card-payment what-ifs, transaction lookups). It may quote amounts and percentages, but every one is checked against figures registered by deterministic code (or typed by the user); after one failed revision the response falls back to a built-in answer. Supersedes D-014, D-029, and D-030. | Banning all numbers made nearly every model answer fail validation and fall back to generic copy, and purchase questions never reached the model. Grounding each quoted figure keeps arithmetic in tested integer-cent code while allowing specific, personal coaching. Risk: the regex grounding check covers `$` amounts and `%`, not counts of months, which tools return and the prompt forbids estimating. |
| D-032 | 2026-10-30 | Accepted | Generalize approval-gated proposals to savings-goal contributions and monthly budget limits, both owner-bound, version-bound, single-use, and rechecked against the current value at approval. Add `GET /api/v1/coach/brief`, a deterministic monthly check-in that needs no model call. | Coaching often ends in a budget change, which AGENTS.md requires to be approval-gated. The check-in gives a useful, reliable first screen for the demo even without a model key. |
